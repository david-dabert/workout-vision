# Tried and closed

Read this before proposing any counting, measurement or data idea (CLAUDE.md, R11).
Each line: what was tried, the measured result, why it is closed or parked, and where the evidence lives.
An idea listed here is not proposed again unless new data or a new mechanism changes its premise; say which.
Written 2 October 2026, after two proposals in one hour repeated closed work.

## Counting rules

- **Count a last rep cut by the end of the recording** (1 October). David's sets 7 -> 8 exact; Countix build 271 -> 304. Withdrawn: movements after the set (reaching for the phone, standing up after hip thrusts) pass the same rule; one joint angle cannot tell them apart. Remedy is at capture. `test/real-phone/end-rule/README.md`, iteration 1. Reopened for the 30-second tests only (2 October; new premise: the published protocol counts a rise past halfway when time ends, and the window bounds it): `openRise` in `src/lib/fitness-tests.js`. The general rule stays withdrawn for a rise cut before it returns; a rep cut on its way back was reopened, narrowed, on 3 October (entry below, CUT_RETURN_SHARE).
- **Warn when a recording starts or ends inside a rep** (1 October). Fires on 12 of David's 14 sets, right or wrong. Not built. `test/real-phone/end-rule/README.md`, iteration 2.
- **Either side does the rep** (lunge, front raise; 30 September). Countix build 271 -> 299, but one clip became off by 3+, and a short dropout splits alternating reps (8 raises count 16). Withdrawn. `test/real-phone/either-side/README.md`.
- **A rep's range measured from its rest** (28 September). Parked by David; open reviewer cases (an 18° partial counts 7 for 6). Branches `range-from-rest`, `range-from-rest-review`; STATE.md.
- **Rest band 10 % / 3° -> 5 % / 2°** (2 October). Shipped in PR #51: tempo closer to truth on synthetic sets; one Countix push-up went 2 -> 1. `src/lib/counting/core.ts` comment.

- **Calf raises by the knee-ankle-toe angle** (2 October). Six synthetic sets of 10: in profile 9, 9 and 0 (rises of 35, 40 and 30 degrees), 0 at 60 and 120 degrees of view; the pose model reads the ankle over about twice its true range in profile. Not shipped. Reopen with a new mechanism (heel height, a foot model), not by tuning thresholds. `test/real-phone/ankle/README.md`.

- **End a rep at any lost pose** (2 October, audit FINDING-010). A rise seen before a long gap and a return after it can make one rep whose middle was not seen. Ending the rep at any null after bridging: public build 271 -> 260 exact, 8 sets newly off by 3 or more; synthetic 74 -> 73 exact; the target case still miscounted. Withdrawn: nulls after bridging are far more frequent in real video than the case assumed. Reopen only with a rule bounded by the gap's length and measured on all three gates. `src/lib/counting/__tests__/audit-core.test.ts` keeps the case as an expected failure.
- **Count the side that moves more when both are seen about as well** (2 October, audit FINDING-011). A still arm a little better seen can take the count from the working arm (synthetic: 0 of 8). Choosing the larger angle range when visibilities are within 85 %: public build 271 -> 264 exact, 19 sets newly off by 3 or more; synthetic gate failed (eccentric error 0.5 -> 0.6 s). Withdrawn: on real video the side that moves more is often the noisier one. The case stays as an expected failure in `audit-core.test.ts`.
- **Keep the turn of fast reps through the outlier filter** (3 October, second audit). - **Savitzky–Golay kernels for any window** (6 October, audit of 6 October action 4, B). Above about 33 samples per second the 0.333 s window needs more than the 11 samples the fixed tables held, and the smoothing fell back to 5 samples silently (at 60 sps a 10° one-sample spike kept 4.9° instead of 1.1°). Quadratic coefficients are now generated for any odd window from the closed form (Savitzky & Golay 1964; identical to the table for 5 to 11, `sg-kernel.test.ts`); 3 samples keep the moving average. - **The longest rep bounds moving time, not a pause at the working end** (6 October, audit of 6 October action 5; `WORK_HOLD_CAP_SEC`). MAX_REP_SEC = 8 s bounded a rep's whole duration, so a pause at the working end counted against it: eight squats or lateral raises paused 5 s read 0, and six plain plus two paused read 6 of 8 with full coverage (a confident wrong count, R8). ## Other counters

- **Learned counter** (30 September; small temporal network on 12 joints, trained on Countix build). Its headline, 229 of 447 exact by cross-validation, sums the density inside each clip's labelled window, which the app does not have. Read as the app would (clip cut to its window, 2 s still at each end, the fold model that never saw it, TypeScript inference): 136 exact against the core's 147, within one 359 against 288, off by 3+ 25 against 48, and 12 clips off by 3+ where the core was not (2 October, `test/real-phone/accuracy/learned-span.txt`). David's 14 sets: 8 exact against 7. Its sum runs about half a rep high on Countix held clips; shifting it down half a rep gives Countix 194 exact but David's sets 4 of 14: the two kinds of recording pull a fixed calibration opposite ways. Summing only over the core's reps: Countix 142, David 3 of 14. The variants with holds and time-stretch (LENGTHEN, TIMEBASE) help Countix held clips (230) and break David's sets (2 to 4 of 14). Not ready for the app; what it lacks is phone recordings to train or calibrate on. `src/lib/counting/learned.ts`; `test/real-phone/accuracy/learned-*.txt`.
- **Period counter** (frequency of the smoothed angle). 54 % exact where it answers, 55 % coverage. Measured, not used. `test/real-phone/agreement/agreement.txt`.
- **Pose doubt: holes and coverage** (6 October, swarm review action 1). A set is flagged when an unseen run lasts half its median rep (2 s with fewer than two reps) or under 80 % of its samples are seen, by the refusal's own rule (`src/lib/counting/doubt.js`; thresholds frozen before measuring, UNSOURCED, experimental). Public build half: 195 of 946 counted sets flagged, 33 % exact against 57 % for the others, 12 % off by 3 or more against 3 %; 23 of the 49 sets off by 3 or more flagged. David's sets: the hip thrust 7 for 13 and the squat 7 for 7 flagged, nothing else. A worst-5-second-window rule (under half the window seen) was tried on top and flagged no further set; dropped. Stored with each saved set (`doubt`), printed by both scoreboards, read by no screen: the result screen's use of it waits for David (R8, R10).
- **Agreement of counters as confidence** (1 October). All three agree on 70 of 447 clips, 56 exact (80 %), 1 off by 3+. Closed: not a reliable automatic gate; may enter a confidence model only if it beats a visibility/gaps/exercise baseline on held-out phone sessions. `test/real-phone/agreement/CONCLUSION.md`.
- **Turning points and whole-body signal** (30 September). Neither beats the core overall on Countix. `test/real-phone/accuracy/public-signal.txt`.
- **Confidence from pose coverage** (30 September). Sets with a pose on every sample: 42 % exact against 29 % below 0.9; a weak signal alone. `test/real-phone/accuracy/public-confidence.txt`.
- **A state-model counter** (3 October, after the second audit). Rest, going out, working end, coming back; the most likely path over the set (Viterbi), one rep per full pass; `src/lib/counting/hmm.ts`, research only. It counts the eight 0.7 s curls the core counts as one, and ignores a one-sample glitch. Against the core, set by set (`test/real-phone/accuracy/hmm-eval.txt`): David's sets 7 and 7 exact (3 against 1 off by 3 or more); public build half 220 against 271 exact (123 against 204 off by 3 or more); synthetic sets 59 against 74. A grid of 54 settings moved it by 3 sets at most (`hmm-grid.txt`). Using it only where it and the core differ by 3 or more gained 2 exact sets on one half of the public build sets, none on the other, and lost 4 to 6 synthetic sets (`hmm-hybrid.txt`). Main cause seen: a dip partway through a rise reads as a return and a new rep (Soldier curls, 18 for 7). Not used; the model and its evaluation stay for a next attempt, which needs blind-counted real sets to aim at.
- **Edge re-read for sets of varying range** (5 October, after Astra's review). Before the first counted rep and after the last, the stretch re-read with thresholds from its own range; a cycle found there counted if it carries a share of the counted reps' median range, lasts near their median, and joins the set. Against the core (`scripts/compare-variants.mjs`, public halves A and B, David's and the synthetic sets): at 40 % of the range it fixes the rows it was aimed at (MM-Fit w12 7 -> 10, sets off by 3 or more on rows 4 -> 1) but adds a rep before or after the set elsewhere (exact sets of front raise, triceps, lunge, push-up, and a synthetic curl, 7 -> 8); public A 255 -> 253 exact, synthetic 74 -> 73. At 50 % on the head only, rows still gain (7 -> 9, 6 -> 7) and the synthetic curl still reads 8. At 70 % it no longer reaches the rows (their first reps carry about half the range). Not used: a range share alone cannot tell a shallow first rep from setup movement. A next attempt needs a second discriminator, such as the rhythm of the set (a first rep falls one period before the second; picking up a dumbbell does not).
