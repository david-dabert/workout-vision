# Tried and closed

Read this before proposing any counting, measurement or data idea (CLAUDE.md, R11).
Each line: what was tried, the measured result, why it is closed or parked, and where the evidence lives.
An idea listed here is not proposed again unless new data or a new mechanism changes its premise; say which.

## Counting rules

- **Count a last rep cut by the end of the recording** (1 October). David's sets 7 -> 8 exact; Countix build 271 -> 304. Withdrawn: movements after the set (reaching for the phone, standing up after hip thrusts) pass the same rule; one joint angle cannot tell them apart. Remedy is at capture. `test/real-phone/end-rule/README.md`, iteration 1. The general rule stays withdrawn for a rise cut before it returns; a rep cut on its way back was reopened, narrowed, on 3 October (entry below, CUT_RETURN_SHARE).

## Measures

- **Left/right from any view** (1 October, iteration 3). Side views read the hidden limb as visible (curl SI -87 %). Withdrawn. Shipped instead: lateral raise filmed square on only, within 10 points per set on synthetic bodies. `test/real-phone/symmetry/README.md`, `test/real-phone/synth/synth.txt`.
- **Speed change, first to last rep** (2 October). Within timing noise (synthetic median 15 %, p90 72 %). Hidden everywhere (`SPEED_CHANGE_SHOWN`). `src/components/experience/measures.js`.

- **Where the range and phase-time bias comes from** (6 October, BACKLOG of 6 October step 1; review of 6 October action 9). On the 96 synthetic sets the app read range about 30 % low and phase times 45 to 54 % short. The harness (`analyse.test.ts`) now also runs the app's own pipeline (outlier removal, bridge, Savitzky-Golay, thresholds, placeBoundaries) on landmarks built to give each side exactly its true angle, and splits the error in two (printed in `synth.txt`, not gated): definition = that pipeline on the true angle against the truth; pose = the app on the detected pose against that pipeline. Range: definition 0 % (all sets; -1 to +8 % per lift at the guided view), pose -30 % (-36 % at 0°, -29 % at 90°; guided view: lateral raise -3 %, curl -24 %, press -37 %, squat -16 %). The raw unsmoothed pose angle already holds the loss (e.g. curl from the front: true 122°, raw pose 65°), so neither the truth's definition, the smoothing nor the rep boundaries cause it: it is the pose model's world landmarks, worst where the limb swings along the camera axis. Phase times: definition -28 % concentric, -29 % eccentric, by design: a phase runs from leaving a band of 5 % of the set's range around rest to entering the same band around the held working extreme (REST_BAND_FRACTION), and an eased movement spends about the first and last seventh of each phase inside those bands; the truth times from motion onset to the peak. Pose adds -27 % and -26 % more. Nothing changed in the app: the band is an onset-threshold convention that keeps noise out, and a factor fitted to the synthetic bodies' easing would be a fudge; the pose part cannot be corrected in the measures. Consequence: a range read beside a reference would be about 16 to 24 % low at the guided view on the two Beta lifts measured here (squat, curl), so the reference band on the rep wave stays behind a flag that is off (`REFERENCE_BAND_SHOWN`). A tempo states the time between those bands, about 0.7 of the full movement time on clean poses. Measures-only change in the harness: no count moved (synthetic 74 -> 74; scoreboard and public build unchanged, see the commit).
