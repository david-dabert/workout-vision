This file replaces the hand-typed table PR #189 committed under the same name.

Every cell comes from the Commons API (category, licence, author, duration, resolution, size) or from the app's pose pipeline in Chromium (full body, body height, hip motion).
Nothing is typed in; the rows are the jobs' printed tables, merged.

- Licences kept: CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA; nothing NC or ND. Files over 60 MB are left out.
- Full body: share of samples where landmarks 11 to 28 (shoulders to feet) are all seen with visibility above 0.5. A strict bar: it fails whenever a foot or a hand leaves the frame.
- Body height: median height of the landmarks as a share of the frame. Above 1.0 means landmarks placed outside the frame: a close-up or a false detection.
- Hip motion: median frame-to-frame shift of the hip midpoint. It mixes camera movement with the subject's own travel; it does not say whether the camera is fixed.
- Animals: not read (the pose model is human); metadata only.
- Errors on `De-*.ogg`: audio files (word pronunciations) filed in the same categories; ffmpeg finds no video stream in them.
- Camera (Antigravity) and Person seen whole (Antigravity): from Antigravity's visual check of the 30 files PR #189 named (issue #190, comment 6184632164), joined by file name by `join.py`, not retyped. Six frames per file at whole-second seeks.
  Camera compares the background across the six frames; "moving" includes cuts between shots. Person seen whole counts any person, not only the athlete (officials in high jump 01, staff in the TdF clip).
  Trot animated (1.0 s) and the dog gait clip (5.0 s) cannot give six distinct frames; Breeze is a single frame. Files outside PR #189's list have no visual check.
