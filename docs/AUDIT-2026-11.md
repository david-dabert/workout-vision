## The three findings that matter

These are defects against R8 (never display a number the app cannot measure) and gate 7. They are recorded here with their evidence and carried by card 013; this card changes no code.

1. **The session report shows a coach's prescription from the readings that were switched off.** `src/components/experience/report-sheet.js` calls `analyzeMovementOptimization` and `analyzeContinuousBiomechanics` (`src/lib/coaching/movementOptimization.ts`) whenever `MEASURES_SHOWN` is true, which it is. `src/components/experience/Report.jsx` and `src/components/experience/report-pdf.js` then print a prescription, a limiting factor, optimisation metrics, coaching points and a `.bvh` download. `UNVALIDATED_BIOMECHANICS_SHOWN = false` guards the replay and the result screen, not the report. When a set has a count and no per-rep data, the sheet builds reps of 90 degrees and 2.0 seconds each and analyses those (`report-sheet.js`, the `fallbackReps` line). Read in the code; not opened on a screen this hour.
2. **The home offers « Profil athlète & Calibration » in production.** `src/components/experience/Choice.jsx` renders the row with no switch; it opens `src/components/experience/BiomechanicalRetentionQuiz.jsx`, which writes « Fémurs longs détectés » and a femur-to-torso ratio of 1.09 from the height the person typed (175 cm or more), and promises a « Dépense calorique exacte ». PR #368 delayed the quiz on the result screen; the row on the home stayed.
3. **`#equine`, `#veterinary`, `#sports` and the eight sport addresses open in production.** Card 008 removed their rows from the home; `src/App.jsx` still serves the addresses without `showUnmeasuredRoutes()`. Opened in the production build on 12 November: `#equine` shows « Le trot, foulée par foulée », `#golf` shows « Un putt, filmé tel quel ». `#coaches`, `#buddies`, `#ergonomics`, `#chat`, `#showcase`, `#volleyball` and `#live` return to the home, as intended.
