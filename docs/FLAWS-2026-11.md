# Flaw register, 13 November 2026

Written by Claude on David's order of 13 November 2026 ("bring back our methodology and think tank to pinpoint every single flaw in the app and in the entire code base"), from main at `7963ba48`.
"Every single flaw" cannot be promised; this register is what the method finds: machine sweeps for what a tool can see, and the review board of METHOD.md for what a user meets. Each row has its evidence. A flaw not listed here is not proven absent.
Companion to `docs/AUDIT-2026-11.md` (what exists and what is reachable). This file is what is wrong.

Severity: **S1** a live breach of a rule, or a screen that can mislead a user; **S2** a weakness of the core or of the method; **S3** debt that costs time but misleads no one.
Lens (METHOD.md, the review board): **K** Karpathy, measured on data not tuned on; **B** Black, the screen says only what the eval supports; **M** McGill, Contreras, Hewett, a named reference that separates good from bad; **C** Carmack, the smallest change, nothing unreachable left; **R** Rams, Ive, each element once and removable.
Verdict: **card** (a card is written or exists), **lab**, **leave**, **David** (only he can decide).

## What the board concludes

- The honesty work is not finished: three screens still claim more than the app measures (#2, #3, #4). They are cheap to fix and go first.
- The deepest flaw is #1 with #14: the one number that defines accuracy for R16 may be measured on a gate that no longer ships, and on the gate it does measure, "sure" is wrong 4 times in 10. Until that number is right and rising, every feature on top is built on sand.
- The method has a hole in its own gate (#5): "merge on green" and "David confirms on his iPhone" cannot both hold while he sleeps. One of the two rules must give, and only David can choose which.
