## Executive Summary

Fitness influencers and strength coaches juggle an average of 3 to 4 fragmented applications daily to run their businesses and track workouts:
1. One app for logging sets and weight (Hevy, Strong).
2. One app for client communication and habit tracking (Trainerize, TrueCoach).
3. One app for high-aesthetic social media video creation (Instagram, CapCut).
4. One app or spreadsheet for scientific progression and periodization (RP Hypertrophy, Excel).

Workout Vision dissolves this fragmentation by reverse-engineering the core functionality of all 20 platforms into an open, on-device, zero-cost architecture for athletes and coaches.
