## Intended purpose (regulatory seat; for the written opinion, not a legal opinion)
"WorkoutVision lets a physiotherapist choose exercises from a video library, set repetitions, sets and their own written instructions, and send them as a link, QR code or PDF sheet. The person can watch the videos and play a video of themselves beside the physiotherapist's. The software does not measure, analyse, score or interpret the movement or health of anyone. It reports nothing back to the physiotherapist and provides no information for diagnosis, treatment or monitoring. It is not a medical device."
Questions the opinion must answer: (1) whether pose estimation and time alignment used only for a side-by-side display is "action on data" (MDCG 2019-11 Rev.1); (2) whether the audience and named tests create a medical purpose despite the disclaimer; (3) the class of a verdict or a share of range against the healthy side, and its evidence; (4) whether a verdict computed but hidden, stored on the phone, and dormant class IIa code in the bundle matter; (5) GDPR: controller of the link payload and patient videos, a DPIA (Art. 35), HDS; (6) whether a pilot clinic is putting into service or a clinical investigation (Art. 62).
