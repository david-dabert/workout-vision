# WorkoutVision, the current state map (21 October 2026)

Asked by David on 21 October 2026: score every aspect of the app, go through the whole architecture, and say whether it is worth anything and whether it can become a commercial product.
Written on main at 779ebaa, after the merge of #241.

## How to read the scores

The scale, as David set it and as this map reads it:
- 1: what any competent team would ship, nothing distinctive.
- 4: works, unremarkable.
- 7: good, better than most products on the market.
- 10: the best in the world, the reference R16 names.

Every score comes with the reference it is measured against and with evidence (file and line, command output, or URL).
Counting numbers come only from the scoreboard files, with their date (R13): test/real-phone/accuracy/scoreboard.txt (19 October), public-scoreboard.txt (20 October), test/real-phone/synth/synth.txt (6 October).
Published figures quoted from the repository's research notes are second-hand and marked UNSOURCED where no paper table was checked.

Method: seven readers, each over one subsystem, read-only, each returning scores with evidence; then three adversarial reviewers (a sports scientist, a product engineer, an investor) who re-open the evidence and argue each score down or up; then one completeness critic naming what no reader scored.
The readers' scores stand in the tables below.
Sections marked PENDING await the reviewers and the three remaining readers (code and CI, data and governance, market); the account's usage limit stopped them twice on the night of 20 to 21 October.

## The verdict in one page (provisional until the reviewers' pass)

What the app is today: an honest, well-measured rep counter on one joint angle from a stock on-device pose model, wrapped in a careful phone interface and a disciplined evidence system, surrounded by nine demonstration modules that measure nothing from a real video.

What has real value:
1. The evidence apparatus: labelled sets, public benchmarks in two halves, synthetic sets with exact truth, paired statistics, gates in CI, a record of everything tried. A buyer or partner could qualify any engine with it. No reader found a comparable apparatus in a product of this size.
2. The honest interface: refusals, a confirm state with no grade, a sensitivity flag, a "right when sure" line under every scoreboard. Defensible in a market of over-claiming apps.
3. The on-device privacy architecture, except for the physiotherapy commentary that uploads patient video to a third party.
4. The French copy on the live path and the licence-clean set of 309 exercise figures.

What has no sellable value yet:
1. The count itself: 12 of 15 of David's stored sets exact, 8 of 16 of his real videos, 514 of 1,064 public sets (48 %); right 61 % of the time it shows a public count as sure; the count moves on 28 % of public sets under re-encoding noise.
2. Every measure beyond the count: range of motion reads about 30 % low on synthetic truth, tempo 45 to 54 % short, no angle ever compared with a goniometer or motion capture.
3. The physiotherapy path: zero patient trials, every tolerance UNSOURCED, a medical-device question (MDR) unanswered.
4. The nine breadth modules: pitch decks in code, scrupulously badged as demo.

Commercial reading, provisional: not a product today; a credible foundation for one, if the next six months go to the count and to one measured path, not to breadth.
The single thing that would most change the answer: a counter that is right whenever it says it is sure on real phone video, proven on exam sets the counter never saw.

### 2. The pose and video pipeline (reader mean 3.6)

| Dimension | Score | Reference | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pose model choice and its measured limits | 3 | Optical motion capture for angles; Apple Vision 3D body pose, RTMPose-m or ViTPose+ for landmarks | The default any web team ships: MediaPipe pose_landmarker_full, CPU WASM, IMAGE mode, 15 Hz, detection thresholds lowered and UNSOURCED. | Frame extraction and decoding | 5 | Native AVAssetReader on iOS; FFmpeg for breadth | WebCodecs with hardware decode, variable frame rate kept, rotation, a restart with resume for Safari's decoder failure, a stall watchdog, back-pressure at 5 open frames after an iPhone memory kill, an exact whole-read rule, a playback fallback. | Speed on a phone | 2 | Apple Vision on the Neural Engine, real time; GPU-delegated pose at 30 fps on mid-range Android | 99 to 240 ms a sample on the repository's 4-CPU machine, two to three times the video's length; David's iPhone took over a minute for about 480 samples on 7 October; no per-sample time ever measured on an iPhone; every faster option changes the landmarks and fails R2 against the stored reads. |
| On-device privacy architecture | 6 | Apple's on-device Health processing; a static PWA with no analytics endpoint | Inference, counting, storage and PDF on the device; model and WASM served from the app's origin with SHA-256 checks; a CSP pinned to self plus one named host; identifier-free, opt-out analytics honouring GPC and DNT. The exception: the physiotherapy AI commentary uploads patient video to a third-party model, and ARCHITECTURE.md does not say so. |
| Distance from the best mobile pose stacks | 2 | Apple Vision 3D pose on the Neural Engine; RTMPose or RTMO on CoreML; OpenCap Monocular at 4.8 degrees against motion capture | A 2023-era model on single-thread CPU at 15 Hz, no GPU, no WebGPU, no Neural Engine. The alternatives were benchmarked with discipline and declined under R2; the gate compares new engines against counts tuned to the old one, which structurally prevents adopting a better landmark source. |

Strengths: the measured failure anatomy; the decoder hardening; the privacy default.
Gaps: no measurement on an iPhone of anything; lifter selection on multi-person frames unsolved; no stored set with true angles holds image landmarks, so range fixes cannot be proven.

## What the readers agree on

1. The one asset no reader doubted is the evidence apparatus and the honesty rules. Every reader called it rare or distinctive.
2. The one failure every reader returned to is the count's dependence on a pose model whose errors are measured, unfixed, and locked in by the gate that compares every new engine against counts tuned to the old one.
