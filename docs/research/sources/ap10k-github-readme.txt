Keypoint Definition (17 keypoints):
1: Left Eye, 2: Right Eye
3: Nose, 4: Neck
5: Root of Tail
6: Left Shoulder, 7: Left Elbow, 8: Left Front Paw
9: Right Shoulder, 10: Right Elbow, 11: Right Front Paw
12: Left Hip, 13: Left Knee, 14: Left Back Paw
15: Right Hip, 16: Right Knee, 17: Right Back Paw

Unguligrade Anatomical Note:
In quadrupeds and especially unguligrades (horses, cattle, deer), the limbs are elongated. The standard AP-10K 17-keypoint quadruped template defines hind limb joints as:
- 'hip' denotes the anatomical stifle (knee equivalent)
- 'knee' denotes the anatomical hock (ankle/calcaneus equivalent)
- 'paw' denotes the hoof / ground contact point below the fetlock
None of the 17 keypoints correspond to the withers (garrot) or the tuber sacrale (croup / pointe de la fesse).
