Key Primary Statistics for France & Personal Training Model:
- Footprint in France:
  - Total clubs in France: 816 clubs (out of 1,402 group-wide clubs as of year-end 2023; reached > 860 clubs during 2024). France is Basic-Fit's largest country market.
  - Total members in France: ~2.45 million to 2.6 million members.
- ARPU (Average Revenue Per User) & Yield:
  - Average monthly subscription yield per member: 23.53 € (up from 22.86 € in 2022).
  - Premium membership adoption: Over 44% of members subscribed to the "Premium" tier (allowing pass-sharing and digital access).
- Personal Training Model (Club Revenue & Employment Structure):
  - Zero employed personal trainers: Basic-Fit does not employ personal trainers as salaried staff in France.
  - Freelance / Concession Model: Personal trainers operate strictly as independent third-party entrepreneurs (micro-entreprises or SARL/SASU).
  - Rent / Facility Access Fee:
    - Independent personal trainers pay a fixed monthly rent or licensing fee directly to Basic-Fit to obtain exclusive right to solicit and train clients on the gym floor.
    - Standard monthly rental fee in France ranges from 500 € to 1,500 € per trainer per club (depending on club size, footfall tier, and metropolitan density).
    - Basic-Fit records these rental streams under "Other club revenue" (which totalled 38.6 million € in 2023, up 22%).
    - The trainer charges their own rates directly to gym members (typically 45 € to 70 €/hour) and retains 100% of their coaching fee above the gym rental overhead.
- Underpinned Financial Metric:
  - Underlying EBITDA less rent: 261 million € (Group level, 2023).
