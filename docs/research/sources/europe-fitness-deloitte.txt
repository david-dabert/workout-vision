Key Primary Statistics:
- Total European Health & Fitness Revenues:
  - 31.8 billion € (growth of +14.0% year-on-year, surpassing pre-pandemic levels; broader economic impact including apparel/supplements estimated at 39.1 billion €).
- European Fitness Club Memberships:
  - 75.5 million members (up from 71.4 million in 2022, +5.7% annual growth).
  - Penetration rate: Approximately 8.3% of the total European population (higher in Northern Europe: Sweden/Norway > 20%, UK/Germany ~12-14%, France ~9.2%).
  - Strategic target: EuropeActive declared goal of 100 million members in European fitness clubs by 2030.
- European Fitness Facilities / Clubs:
  - 67,515 commercial and non-profit fitness clubs operating across Europe (+2.1% net expansion).
- Top 3 Fitness Operators in Europe by Members:
  1. Basic-Fit: > 3.8 million members (expanding to > 4.2M in 2024 across 6 countries).
  2. PureGym: > 1.9 million members (UK, Denmark, Switzerland).
  3. RSG Group: > 1.5 million members (McFit, Gold's Gym, John Reed).
- Mergers & Acquisitions:
  - Active consolidation with 16 major corporate M&A transactions recorded in 2023, reflecting investor demand for scalable low-cost and hybrid digital models.
