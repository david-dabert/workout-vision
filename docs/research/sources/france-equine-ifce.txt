2. FFE (Fédération Française d'Équitation) — Bilan annuel des licences et de la pratique équestre
Fetched: 2026-10-14T07:55:00Z

Key Primary Statistics:
- Number of Riders & Licenses:
  - Total FFE Licences: 648,328 licensed equestrians (ranking equestrianism as the 3rd largest sports federation in France, behind football and tennis).
  - Feminisation: 83.4% of licensed riders are women / girls (#1 female sport federation in France).
  - Total active horse riders (including non-licensed regular / leisure riders): Estimated at 2.1 million to 2.5 million participants.
- Equine Population & Infrastructure:
  - Total Equines in France: ~1,020,000 horses, ponies, donkeys, and racehorses.
  - Riding Facilities: 9,560 equestrian establishments (centres équestres, poney-clubs, écuries de propriétaires).
  - Direct Employment: 17,800 full-time equivalent employees working directly in equestrian centers and breeding structures (total 66,000 active persons in broader equine sector including racing and agriculture).
- Economic Weight:
  - Direct financial flows of the French equine sector: 6.2 billion € annually (agriculture, sport, leisure, betting, export, equipment).
  - Sports & Leisure Equestrian Turnover: 1.1 billion € directly generated by equestrian riding centers.
- Annual Spend per Horse Owner:
  - Average annual expenditure per horse: 3,500 € to 7,200 € / year.
  - Monthly Livery / Boarding (Pension écurie): 300 € to 650 € / month (up to 900 € in Île-de-France).
  - Farrier (Maréchalerie): 80 € to 120 € every 6–8 weeks (~600 € to 900 € / year).
  - Veterinary & Routine Care (Vaccinations, dental rasping, deworming): 450 € to 850 € / year (excluding emergency colic or lameness diagnostic workups which average 1,500 € to 4,000 €).
