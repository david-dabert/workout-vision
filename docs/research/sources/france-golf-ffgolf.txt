Key Primary Statistics:
- Number of Licensed Golfers in France:
  - 2022: 441,961
  - 2023: 445,306 (all-time historic peak)
  - 2024: 442,536
  - 2025: 446,547 (latest published census)
- Growth Dynamics:
  - 10-year cumulative expansion: +9.6% net growth over the past decade.
  - Golf density: 63.8 licenses per 10,000 French inhabitants.
- Demographic & Socioeconomic Profile:
  - Average Age: 54.2 years (Men: 53.1 years; Women: 57.4 years).
  - Gender Split: 26.2% female, 73.8% male.
  - Loyalty & Retention: 83.2% annual licence renewal rate; > 91% long-term member retention rate.
  - Socio-Professional Category (CSP): Disproportionate over-representation of CSP+ (Executives, higher intellectual professions, company heads, independent practitioners representing > 58% of adult players).
  - Geographic Concentration: Highly concentrated in high-disposable-income regions (> 110 licenses / 10,000 residents in Hauts-de-Seine, Yvelines, Landes, Pyrénées-Atlantiques, Alpes-Maritimes, Var).
- Facility Network:
  - 742 golf courses across metropolitan France (comprising 18-hole championship courses, 9-hole facilities, and compact executive driving ranges).
