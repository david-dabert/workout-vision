Domain: Gluteal Tendinopathy, Greater Trochanteric Pain Syndrome & Hip Osteoarthritis
Fetched: 2026-10-14T07:35:00Z

Summary of Standard Clinical Exercises:
1. Clamshell (Gluteus Medius / Deep External Rotators):
   - Starting Position: Side-lying with hips flexed to 45 degrees, knees bent to 90 degrees, heels glued together.
   - Movement: Keeping feet touching, rotate top knee upward toward ceiling without allowing pelvis to roll backward, hold 2 seconds, lower slowly.
   - Prescription: 3 sets of 12–15 reps per side.
   - Repetition Criteria: Top knee abducts to >= 35–45 degrees without posterior pelvic tilt or lumbar rotation.
2. Side-Lying Hip Abduction:
   - Starting Position: Side-lying with bottom leg slightly bent for stability, top leg straight and in slight extension (~10 degrees posterior to torso line) with toes pointing forward or slightly down.
   - Movement: Lift top leg upward to ~30–45 degrees abduction, hold 2 seconds, lower under control.
   - Prescription: 3 sets of 10–12 reps per leg.
   - Repetition Criteria: Abduction to >= 30 degrees without hip flexing forward or pelvic tilting.
3. Glute Bridge (Bilateral Supine Pelvic Elevation):
   - Starting Position: Supine on mat, knees bent to 90 degrees, feet flat on floor hip-width apart, arms resting by sides.
   - Movement: Squeeze gluteal muscles, press through heels, lift pelvis until thighs and torso form a straight diagonal line, hold 2–3 seconds, lower under control.
   - Prescription: 3 sets of 10–15 reps, tempo 2-2-2.
   - Repetition Criteria: Attainment of 0 degrees hip extension without lumbar hyperextension.
4. Bird-Dog (Quadruped Contralateral Limb Extension):
   - Starting Position: On all fours (quadruped), hands under shoulders, knees under hips, spine in neutral alignment.
   - Movement: Simultaneously extend right arm forward and left leg backward until both are parallel to floor, hold 3 seconds, return to neutral, alternate sides.
   - Prescription: 3 sets of 8–10 reps per side.
   - Repetition Criteria: Limb elevated to horizontal without pelvic rotation or lumbar sagging.
5. Donkey Kick (Quadruped Hip Extension):
   - Starting Position: Quadruped position, knees bent to 90 degrees.
   - Movement: Keeping knee bent at 90 degrees, press bottom of foot up toward ceiling extending hip, hold 2 seconds at top, return knee without touching ground.
   - Prescription: 3 sets of 12 reps per side.
   - Repetition Criteria: Thigh reaches parallel to floor with gluteal contraction, zero lumbar arching.
6. Monster Walk / Lateral Band Walk:
   - Starting Position: Standing in semi-squat athletic stance (20–30 degrees knee flexion) with resistance band placed around ankles or above knees.
   - Movement: Step laterally maintaining constant tension on band and feet pointing straight ahead, control trailing leg.
   - Prescription: 3 sets of 10–15 steps per direction.
   - Repetition Criteria: Controlled lateral steps with knees tracking over toes, no knee valgus collapse.
7. Single Leg Glute Bridge:
   - Starting Position: Supine on mat, one knee bent with foot flat, opposite leg lifted off floor (straight or flexed at hip).
   - Movement: Drive through planted heel to raise pelvis until hip is fully extended, hold 2 seconds, lower slowly.
   - Prescription: 3 sets of 8–10 reps per side.
   - Repetition Criteria: Pelvis remains horizontally level during lift without dropping on unsupported side.
8. Hip Flexor Stretch (Kneeling Lunge Hold):
   - Starting Position: Half-kneeling on mat (back knee down, front knee bent to 90 degrees with foot flat).
   - Movement: Perform posterior pelvic tilt (tucking pelvis under), gently shift body weight forward until stretch felt in front of back hip, hold statically.
   - Prescription: 3 holds of 30 seconds per side.
   - Repetition Criteria: Static upright posture with neutral lumbar spine maintained throughout hold.
