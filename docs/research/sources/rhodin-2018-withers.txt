Title: Vertical movement symmetry of the withers in horses with induced forelimb and hindlimb lameness at trot
Journal: Equine Veterinary Journal, 2018, Volume 50, Issue 6, Pages 818–824
Authors: M. Rhodin, E. Persson-Sjödin, A. Egenvall, F. M. Serra Bragança, T. Pfau, L. Roepstorff, M. A. Weishaupt, M. H. Thomsen, P. R. van Weeren, E. Hernlund

Abstract:
Background: Vertical movement asymmetries of the head and pelvis are routinely measured to objectively detect lameness in horses. However, compensatory head nodding caused by hindlimb lameness frequently confounds clinical forelimb evaluations.
Objectives: To investigate whether vertical movement symmetry of the withers can assist in differentiating primary forelimb lameness from compensatory head nod caused by hindlimb lameness.
Results: Withers vertical movement asymmetry showed high agreement with primary forelimb lameness and did not demonstrate the false-positive compensatory asymmetries seen in head vertical movement during induced hindlimb lameness.
Conclusion: The withers provides an essential reference landmark for distinguishing primary forelimb lameness from compensatory movements in horses.
