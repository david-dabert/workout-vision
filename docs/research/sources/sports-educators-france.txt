Key Primary Statistics:
- Total Active Professional Cards (Cartes Professionnelles EAPS):
  - Approximately 110,000 to 125,000 active registered cards across France (statutory 5-year validity with mandatory criminal background / casier B2 check under Article L. 212-1 of the Code du Sport).
  - Annual renewals and new declarations: ~25,000/year.
- Status Breakdown:
  - Salaried (Associations & Clubs): ~48% (predominantly in sports federations, municipal sports directions, and commercial fitness centers).
  - Self-employed / Freelance (Micro-entrepreneurs / Entreprises individuelles): ~52% (growing rapidly from 34% in 2017 to over 50% post-2021).
  - Commercial Gyms / Fitness Chains: Approximately 22,000 personal trainers and group fitness coaches operate inside private fitness centers.
- Economics of Independent Personal Trainers in France:
  - Typical Hourly Rate (Séance 1 heure):
    - Regional cities: 40 € to 60 € / hour.
    - Paris / Île-de-France: 60 € to 100 € / hour.
  - Active Client Roster Size:
    - Typical full-time independent trainer maintains an active roster of 15 to 25 weekly clients (representing 20–35 billable sessions per week).
  - Client Churn Rate:
    - Average quarterly client churn: 30% to 45%.
    - Average client retention lifetime: 4 to 7 months for 1-on-1 private coaching.
  - Principal Attrition Factor: Administrative friction (invoicing, scheduling, tracking workout logs) and client drop-off due to perceived lack of objective progress measurement.
