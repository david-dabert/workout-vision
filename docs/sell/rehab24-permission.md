# Permission request to the authors of REHAB24-6 (card 063, track B)

To send by David to the corresponding author of Černek, Sedmidubsky, Budikova, "REHAB24-6: Physical Therapy Dataset for Analyzing Pose Estimation Methods", SISAP 2024 (Masaryk University; the address is in the paper). The dataset's page states it is "for academic or non-profit organization noncommercial research"; WorkoutVision intends to sell, so the use needs their written yes.

## The message (English, to send as is)

Subject: Permission to evaluate an exercise application on REHAB24-6

Dear Dr Sedmidubsky,

I am developing WorkoutVision, an application that counts and compares exercise repetitions from a phone video, with the video kept on the phone.
Your REHAB24-6 dataset is the only public set I have found that pairs physiotherapist correctness labels with optical motion capture.
I would like to use it to measure, once, how often our comparison agrees with your physiotherapist's labels, and how far our joint angles sit from your motion capture.
The data would stay on our machines, would never be redistributed, published or used to train a model, and only aggregate results would be reported, with full citation of your paper.
Because WorkoutVision is intended to become a commercial product, I am asking for your written permission before any use.
I would gladly share the results with your group.

With my thanks,

David Dabert
