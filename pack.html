Video packer

Pick the videos of your sets. Each is made small on this phone (the pictures the app counts: 15 a second, 640 px, no sound) and kept on this phone until you make the file. Nothing is uploaded.

For each video, choose the exercise and type the reps you counted. This page never shows a count of the app's, so your counts are blind. The exercise and view carry to the videos below until you change one.

Keep the screen on and this page open while it packs. If a large pick stalls, pick about 20 at a time: each pick is added below the others.
