## What this says

The edge misses that decide (set02 hip thrust, lateral raise 9, and on video the chest row) are all a last rep the video stops on before its return, which edges.test.ts pins as not counted and David's labels count.
The pinned cases are regular synthetic sets, so a rule that counts these three must break edges.test.ts: the convention, not a missing discriminator, is the obstacle.
The one discriminator TRIED.md left open for edges, the rhythm of the set (5 October), was measured below and fails.

## The change tried: a cut last rep counted only when it starts in the set's rhythm

How it differs from the three withdrawn rules: those judged the last excursion by its angle alone (reaching the working end; 70 % of the return; a share of the median range). This one judges it by time: the excursion must leave rest one period after the last counted rep left it (the median gap between rep onsets, within a tolerance), the second discriminator TRIED.md names (edge re-read, 5 October). A movement after the set (standing up, reaching for the phone) was expected to come off the beat.
Measured as a post-process of the live core's result, with no change to the core (`RHYTHM=1 npx vitest run test/real-phone/accuracy/rhythm-tail.test.ts`, with `TOL` and `REACH`; the edges.test.ts cases are the two concentric-first cut cases for bicep_curl and lateral_raise at 15 and 30 sps, rebuilt there; two settings, chosen before the run: tolerance 25 % of the period with 50 % of the way to the working threshold; tolerance 15 % with 70 %):
