# A set window marked by the user: measured, 15 October 2026

Proposed by Martin (coach, Elevate Premium Fitness) in David's meeting of 15 October: let the person mark where the set starts and ends before the analysis.
Measured by the count-edges session (session_01KW5NwHTN8gLCJ8Umnj7rc3) with a scratch post-process (summarizeCount on landmarks sliced to the window); the harness is not in the repository. Recorded here by the parent session from that session's report.

## David's sets
No labelled set window exists: the manifests (landmarks, sets-07oct-video) and the set files carry no start or end of the set. The trim cannot be measured on his clips until he marks the windows (R1).
It cannot recover his deciding edge misses (set02 hip thrust 7, lateral raise 9, chest row 6 on video): in each, the video stops before the last rep returns, and a trim only removes footage. At most it could fix the chin-up's last 0.7 s dip (6 for 5), and only if that dip lies outside the set.

## Reading
A user-set window helps with movement around the set (people passing, setup). It cannot add a rep that was not filmed. A window 0.5 s too tight halves the exact count, so any interface must pad the marked window outward by 0.5 to 1 s. Whether it pays on gym video needs David's own sets with the windows he marks.
