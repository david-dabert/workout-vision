# Conclusion, 1 October 2026 (closed)

Reviewed by Astra, relayed by David. No further analysis; no threshold searched.

- On these development clips, agreement selects a smaller subset with higher exact accuracy: all three counters agree on 70 of 447 clips, 56 exact, 14 wrong, 1 off by 3 or more.
- That rejects agreement as a reliable automatic gate under the current expectations. It does not establish that agreement improves confidence on phone recordings.
- With one to three severe errors per rule, there is insufficient evidence to rank the rules' severe-error rates. That does not show the differences are noise.
- The phi coefficients describe correctness associations on the clips both counters answer. Those subsets differ, so they do not rank the counters' independence.
- Agreement stays available for evaluation on phone data. It enters a deployed confidence model only if it adds predictive value beyond a baseline combining visibility, gaps and exercise type, evaluated on the same held-out sessions.
