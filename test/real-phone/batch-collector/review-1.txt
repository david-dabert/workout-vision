DEFECTS

1. A video picked twice on an iPhone is kept twice, but the page says it is kept once.
- Where: /home/user/workout-vision/src/lib/batchCollect.js:47 (`sameFile` compares name, size and lastModified) and the claim at /home/user/workout-vision/src/collect-batch-main.js:90 ("a video picked twice is kept once").
- Scenario: iPhone Safari gives a file picked from Photos a lastModified equal to the moment it was handed over, not the filming date. David picks 12 videos, collects, then picks again with IMG_0412 selected a second time. The second copy gets a new lastModified, so it passes `sameFile` and becomes a new set. Both files carry the same SHA-256 and count, under two set numbers. If both go into the build sets, the scoreboard counts that clip twice.
- The same fact makes the rule "numbered by the files' dates" follow the order of the hand-over on the phone, not the filming order. Only the thumbnails protect against that.
- Smallest failing test (confirmed with node, it returns 2):
  `const a={name:'IMG_0412.MOV',size:10,lastModified:1000}; expect(appendRows(rowsFor([a]),[{...a,lastModified:2000}]).length).toBe(1)`
- Fix direction: drop a duplicate by its sha256 after hashing, or at least do not match on lastModified.

Safari rejects that call with InvalidStateError, which is not an AbortError, so the page writes "Sharing did not go through: use Download." while the first sheet is open and may still succeed.
- Smallest failing test: a Playwright test on collect-batch.html with `navigator.canShare = () => true` and a stubbed `navigator.share` that returns a pending promise on the first call and rejects `new DOMException('x','InvalidStateError')` on the second.
