DEFECTS STILL OPEN OR NEW

1. After a reload, set numbering starts again at 1, and earlier sets are no longer recognised as duplicates.
- Where: /home/user/workout-vision/src/collect-batch-main.js:22 (`rows` lives only in page memory) and /home/user/workout-vision/src/lib/batchCollect.js:65 (`appendRows` numbers from `rows.length + 1`).
- Scenario: David collects and shares sets 1 to 12. He then switches to Photos to check a count. iOS evicts the Safari tab after this memory-heavy run, and the page reloads. He picks the last 8 videos: they become set01 to set08 again. He now holds two `set01_…json.gz` files, and the "numbered as filmed" order is broken.
