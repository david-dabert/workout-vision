# Either side does the rep: withdrawn, 30 September 2026

The change in either-side.patch read the lunge and the front raise on the more worked of the two sides at each
sample. On the Countix build half it moved exact counts from 271 to 299, but one video (nqJ3mAhfj_M, lunge,
label 6) became off by 3 or more, so it fails the public gate. Its first review (review-1.txt) found that the
lunge half never reaches a user (the app offers forward_lunge and the other lunge keys, not lunge), and that a
short dropout of the working arm hands the angle to the resting arm and splits the rep (8 alternating raises
count 16 with a 0.3 s dropout). Withdrawn, and not applied to the core. Kept here as evidence.
