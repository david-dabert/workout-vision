# Filming a prone Y raise: words drafted, not in the app (R10)

Written 8 October 2026, withdrawn the same day. The prone Y raise can be counted by the core (core.ts,
LiftDefinition.lift: the arms' lift against gravity; test/real-phone/synth/motions/lift-angle.txt) but is not offered
(offer.js, NOT_YET_COUNTED), so the Film screen has no step for it. Kept for the day it is offered.

What the renders show, and do not: with the phone upright beside the body, the pose model found the body on 2 of 7
rendered sets (one of the two counted exactly); with the phone on its side, on 11 of 14. The phone on its side was
measured 0.2 to 0.7 m above the floor (the renderer's camera height), not lying on the floor: the only renders with the
camera at floor level (Soldier body) gave no exact count. The line below says "on the floor" and is therefore not what
was measured; it must be re-measured, or reworded, before it is used.

| FR | EN | Where | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calez le téléphone à l'horizontale, au sol, sur le côté : il doit vous voir de profil, des mains aux pieds. | Prop the phone sideways on the floor at your side, so it sees you in profile, hands to feet. | Film screen, step 1, for an exercise read as a lift | withdrawn (8 October): not measured as worded |

Also to settle before it is offered: step 2 ("Au moins de la tête aux hanches dans le cadre") contradicts "des mains
aux pieds" for this exercise, and the refusal's advice when nobody is found ("Posez le téléphone face à vous") is the
opposite of this filming.
