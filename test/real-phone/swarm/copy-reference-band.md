# Copy for David's approval: the reference band on the rep wave (6 October 2026)

Status: approved by David on 6 October 2026, register vous (R10). The band stays behind a flag that is off
(`REFERENCE_BAND_SHOWN`, src/lib/reference-ranges.js): on the synthetic sets the app reads a rep's range 24 % low
on the curl and 16 % low on the squat at the side view (test/real-phone/synth/synth.txt), so a full rep would look
short of the band.

What the band is: a faint stripe behind the angle wave, from the angle a full repetition reaches at its working
end to the angle at rest, for the Beta lifts with a sourced range (curl, squat, Romanian deadlift).

Proposed words, to place in the wave's key beside "out" and "back":

| Where | English | French (approved, vous) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Key, beside the stripe | reference range | amplitude de référence |
| Tap or long-press note | A reference execution from published sources, not a target for you: the right range depends on you, your goal and any injury. | Une exécution de référence tirée de publications, pas un objectif pour vous : la bonne amplitude dépend de vous, de votre objectif et de vos éventuelles blessures. |

Never "optimal", "correct" or "ideal".
